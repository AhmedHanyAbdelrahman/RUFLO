# 1. Executive Summary

Omnikom is building a **Distressed Homeowner Liquidity Network**: a data-driven acquisition, triage, routing, and monetization platform that identifies distressed or motivated homeowners, diagnoses their property situation, compares every realistic exit path, and routes each seller to the best monetization partner.

This is not a call center.  
This is not a basic lead-generation agency.  
This is not a traditional wholesale shop.  
This is not only a listing referral system.

The intended company is a **residential real estate liquidity infrastructure layer** for distressed and motivated homeowners.

The model starts with low-cost outbound calling, in-house county data, and in-house skip tracing. It evolves into:

- A **proprietary Distress Intent Graph** — a living database of homeowner intent signals, outreach outcomes, and transaction results that becomes the company's primary data moat
- An **AI-assisted seller triage layer** (Retell AI + n8n + GPT-4o) that operates 24/7 without human callers
- A **Certified Exit Partner Marketplace** of agents, investors, lenders, and service providers who pay for prioritized access to pre-qualified seller opportunities
- A **SaaS subscription layer** (Seller OS for Partners; Liquidity Map data subscriptions) that generates recurring revenue independent of transaction volume
- A **Nurture Network** — the pre-intent seller database accumulated from every "not now" conversation, licensed to institutional buyers as a proprietary data product

**The proprietary Distress Intent Graph and the Certified Partner Marketplace are the two mechanisms that drive the company from a transaction-revenue business (2–4× EBITDA multiple) to a data-and-marketplace platform (8–15× ARR multiple).**

The core idea:

> Homeowners come confused. Omnikom shows them options. Partners compete to solve the problem. Omnikom owns the intelligence, the workflow, and the flow of opportunities.

**Legal operating structure:** Three-layer entity — overseas holding entity → Wyoming/Delaware US LLC → Licensed US Designated Broker (eXp Realty or equivalent all-50-states broker). All licensed real estate activity is performed by US-based licensed partners. All non-licensed activity (data ops, calling, CRM management, AI workflows) is performed by the overseas team.

## Compliance Doctrine

Every workflow that touches a distressed seller operates within a compliance-first protocol. Pre-foreclosure contacts follow California CC §2945 (Foreclosure Consultant Act). FinCEN beneficial ownership is collected at offer acceptance for all cash or entity purchases. No referral fee is paid to any licensed professional in violation of RESPA Section 8 or California RPC Rule 7.2(b). All overseas team members perform non-licensed functions only — all licensed real estate activity is performed by US-based licensed partners under the designated broker. Compliance is not a legal cost — it is a competitive moat, because non-compliant competitors will be eliminated by enforcement.

## Avatar 1: Equity-Rich but Cash-Poor Owner

**Profile:** Owns a valuable property but has limited liquidity. High equity; low cash flow. Behind on bills, taxes, maintenance, or property expenses.

**Real Pain:** "I have wealth on paper, but I am financially squeezed."

**Fears:** Losing equity; being lowballed; embarrassment; not understanding options; making the wrong decision under pressure.

**What They Need:** A clear comparison between keeping, selling, listing, refinancing, or accepting an offer.

**Omnikom Solution — Seller Options Review:**
- Estimated retail value (CMA)
- As-is value estimate
- Net proceeds comparison by exit path
- Timeline comparison
- Risk comparison
- Recommended next step

**Best UVP:** "Before you make a rushed decision, compare every way to protect your equity."

**Best Script Angle:** "Would it help to compare what you could net by listing, selling as-is, or holding before you decide?"

**Priority Exit:** Novation or retail listing (highest net; protects equity)

## Avatar 4: Pre-Foreclosure Owner

**Profile:** Has a Notice of Default, trustee-sale risk, missed payments, or an approaching foreclosure timeline. AB 2424 (eff. Jan 1, 2025) extends NOD-to-auction to 111–266 days; sellers have more time than they believe.

**Real Pain:** "I need time, clarity, and a way to protect what equity I have left."

**Fears:** Losing the home; losing equity; scams; legal confusion; public embarrassment; pressure from aggressive buyers.

**What They Need:** Safe, compliant, non-legal options review.

**Omnikom Solution:**
- Foreclosure timeline education (not legal advice)
- Options comparison: keep, sell, list, fast offer, talk to lender, talk to attorney
- Timeline-aware routing to licensed partner

**Best UVP:** "Compare your options before the timeline gets tighter."

**Best Script Angle:** "We are not here to pressure you or give legal advice. We help owners compare property options so they can make a cleaner decision."

**Priority Exit:** Fast as-is sale or wholesale (urgency-driven; preserve remaining equity)

**COMPLIANCE WALL — MANDATORY:**
- California CC §2945 (Foreclosure Consultant Act): If Omnikom or any partner enters an agreement to assist the homeowner in avoiding foreclosure, Omnikom is classified as a "Foreclosure Consultant" — subject to DOJ registration, $100K surety bond, 5-day rescission right, and felony exposure for violations.
- **Callers provide the options review ONLY.** No purchase agreements on pre-foreclosure properties without licensed broker in the transaction and full CC §2945 compliance review.
- Script branch for this avatar: *"I want to be upfront — we are not attorneys and this is not legal advice. We help owners compare their property options before making a decision. If you want, I can schedule a free comparison call with our team."*
- Route any homeowner who wants to discuss their specific foreclosure timeline to a licensed housing counselor or attorney.

# 14. Data and Intelligence Model

## The Distress Intent Graph — Architecture

The Distress Intent Graph (DIG) is Omnikom's primary proprietary asset. It is a living graph combining:

**Layer 1: Public Signal Data**
- Tax delinquency records (county tax rolls)
- Notice of Default filings (county recorder)
- Trustee sale recordings
- Probate / estate filings (superior court)
- Divorce filings with real property (superior court — PropertyRadar court-sourced)
- Vacant property indicators (utility shutoff, USPS vacancy flag, DealMachine AI analysis)
- Code violation records (city/county)
- MLS expired / canceled / price-reduced listings (REDX)
- Absentee / out-of-state ownership (assessor records)

**Layer 2: Enrichment Data**
- Skip trace: phone, email, mailing address (PropStream / BatchLeads)
- Equity estimate (PropStream AVM)
- Ownership tenure (assessor)
- Property condition estimate (DealMachine Street View AI)
- Mortgage balance estimate (PropStream)
- Portfolio ownership flag (multiple properties by same owner)

**Layer 3: Outreach History Data**
- Dial attempts; answer rate; date of last contact
- Call outcome disposition (qualified / not now / not interested / DNC)
- Objection history (price; timing; not selling)
- Motivation score (1–10 from ISA assessment)
- Exit preference stated (retail / as-is / investor)
- Follow-up timer (30 / 60 / 90 / 180-day re-engagement)

**Layer 4: Transaction Outcome Data**
- Exit path taken
- Omnikom net revenue per deal
- Partner who closed
- Days from first contact to close
- County; property condition; distress type
- Seller satisfaction score

# 18. Compliance Framework — Complete Stack

This is not a checklist. This is a mandatory operating layer. Every workflow, every deal, every partner interaction must run through this framework.

## Tier 1: Federal Compliance

### TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act)
- **Manual dialing:** No restriction on manually dialing numbers not on DNC
- **Auto-dialing (Retell AI):** Requires prior express written consent OR the number must not be on the DNC registry AND must not be a wireless number without consent
- **Practical protocol:** Retell AI batch calls are placed to numbers sourced from public records (absentee, tax delinquent, probate). These are not subscribers — they are property owners identified through public records. This is legal for manual dialing; ATDS rules apply to auto-dialers. Consult TCPA counsel on Retell AI classification before scale.
- **DNC scrub:** Every calling list must be scrubbed against: Federal DNC registry + State DNC registries + Internal DNC + Litigator scrub (BEFORE every Retell AI batch — non-negotiable)
- **A2P 10DLC:** All SMS campaigns must use 10DLC registered numbers. Registration takes 3–4 weeks. Begin Day 1.
- **Opt-out:** Every SMS must include "Reply STOP to opt out." Every call campaign must log opt-outs immediately to internal DNC.

### FinCEN Residential Real Estate Rule (Effective March 1, 2026)
- **Scope:** All non-financed (cash / entity) purchases of residential real property
- **Omnikom Protocol:**
  1. At offer acceptance on any all-cash or entity deal: trigger UBO collection form via n8n
  2. Collect: beneficial owner name, DOB, address, government ID for all owners with 25%+ interest
  3. Deliver complete UBO documentation to title company BEFORE closing
  4. ClosingLock for encrypted wire instruction delivery
  5. Deal desk manager verifies UBO documentation before any Omnikom commission disbursement

### RESPA Section 8 (Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act)
- **Prohibition:** No fee, kickback, or thing of value in exchange for a referral of business incident to a federally-related mortgage loan settlement service
- **Impact:** Omnikom CANNOT receive referral fees from: title companies, lenders, mortgage brokers, or settlement service providers on federally-related transactions
- **Compliant structures:**
  - ABA (Affiliated Business Arrangement): ownership interest in the service provider with full disclosure
  - MSA (Marketing Services Agreement): fair-market-value payment for legitimate, documented marketing services (not just referrals)
- **Action:** All title and lender partnerships must be reviewed by a RESPA attorney before any fee-sharing arrangement is signed.

## Tier 2: California State Compliance

### CC §2945 — Foreclosure Consultant Act
- **Scope:** Any person who, for compensation, performs any service for a homeowner in connection with avoiding foreclosure
- **Exposure:** Felony; $25K fine; 4 years prison
- **Omnikom Protocol:**
  - Callers provide information and comparison only — they do NOT provide any service to help avoid foreclosure
  - No purchase agreements on pre-foreclosure properties without: (a) licensed broker in transaction, (b) CC §2945 disclosure form signed, (c) 5-day rescission right honored
  - Route any homeowner asking for help with their specific foreclosure to licensed housing counselor or HUD-approved agency

### CC §1695 — Home Equity Purchase Act (HESCA)
- **Scope:** Purchase of a property in foreclosure (NOD filed) from an equity seller
- **Requirements:** 5-day rescission right; specific contract language; 2-year rescission period if violations
- **Omnikom Protocol:**
  - All contracts on NOD properties must include HESCA-compliant language
  - 5-day cooling-off period must be honored (no closings within 5 business days of signing)
  - All NOD purchase agreements reviewed by real estate attorney before execution

### BPC §10131 / §10137 — DRE License Requirements
- **Scope:** Systematic solicitation or negotiation of real estate transactions for compensation requires DRE broker/salesperson license
- **Impact:** Omnikom callers performing non-licensed activity (information, comparison, lead qualification) are exempt. But: any activity that constitutes "negotiating" a transaction requires a licensed principal.
- **Omnikom Protocol:**
  - Callers: book appointment only; do not negotiate price; do not discuss offer terms
  - ISA: deliver SOR information only; do not negotiate; do not represent either party
  - Deal desk: coordinates — but licensed partner executes all negotiation and contract preparation
  - Novation operators: must hold DRE license or operate under licensed broker umbrella

### California RPC Rule 7.2(b) — Attorney Referral Fees
- **Prohibition:** Attorneys in California may not give or receive compensation for referrals
- **Impact:** Probate attorneys, divorce attorneys, estate attorneys CANNOT receive cash referral fees
- **Compliant structure:** Reciprocal referral agreement only. Attorney refers property owner to Omnikom; Omnikom refers legal needs to attorney. No cash exchange for the referral itself.
- **All attorney partnership agreements reviewed by legal counsel before execution**

## Tier 3: Operational Compliance Protocols

### Daily Operations
- DNC scrub: Before every Retell AI batch (automated via n8n → DNC.com API)
- Litigator scrub: Before every Retell AI batch (automated via n8n → Contact Center Compliance)
- Opt-out logging: Real-time to internal DNC upon any opt-out event
- Call recording: Compliant with California 2-party consent law (all-party consent required in CA; disclose at call start)
- Call time controls: No calls before 8AM or after 8PM local time for recipient

### Partner Payment Compliance
- No referral fee paid to any licensed professional without verifying RESPA / RPC 7.2(b) compliance
- All 60/40 split payments must reference a signed partner agreement with the specific deal identified
- No wire transfers without ClosingLock encryption + verified UBO documentation

### Data Privacy
- CCPA: California homeowners whose data is collected must be able to opt out of data sale/sharing
- All list-building from public records (county recorder, court filings) is legal
- Skip trace data from PropStream/BatchLeads: use only for property-related outreach; do not resell individual records

# 29. Risk Register — Complete 12-Risk Model

## Risk 1: Licensing / Compliance

**Risk:** Transaction compensation, referral fees, broker splits, and solicitation activity not legally structured.

**Exposure:** DRE enforcement; DOJ action; civil liability; deal unwinding.

**Mitigation:**
- Broker/legal review before any operations launch
- eXp Realty (or equivalent) broker agreement executed; all licensed activity under broker umbrella
- Compliance protocols live from Day 1 (Section 18)
- Overseas team performs non-licensed functions only

## Risk 2: Partner Concentration

**Risk:** 1–2 partners carry all deal flow. If they leave, revenue collapses.

**Mitigation:**
- Build 20-partner bench within first 30 days
- No single partner to exceed 25% of monthly revenue
- Tiered partner system incentivizes multiple partners to perform for elevation

## Risk 3: Quality Dilution at Scale

**Risk:** Scaling callers creates garbage appointments; partners disengage.

**Mitigation:**
- QA-gate on all Seller Options Reviews before partner assignment
- Bonuses tied to completed SORs and signed opportunities; NOT raw bookings
- Retell AI handles low-quality first-touch; human callers handle warm follow-up
- Caller quality score tracked weekly; underperformers on corrective action plan within 30 days

## Risk 4: Data Decay

**Risk:** County data becomes stale; skip trace invalid; score model feeds bad lists.

**Mitigation:**
- Weekly PropertyRadar + PropStream refresh (automated via n8n)
- Re-skip all records older than 90 days before re-dialing
- Call outcome signals update DIG in real-time
- Litigator scrub before every batch (catches stale numbers that entered litigator lists)

## Risk 5: Channel Dependence (Cold Calling)

**Risk:** TCPA enforcement, AI-dialer regulatory change, or pickup-rate collapse disrupts primary acquisition channel.

**Mitigation:**
- 8-engine diversification plan (Section 15)
- Reinvest 20% of gross revenue monthly into SEO, referral engine, and direct mail
- AI nurture (Engine 3) insulates revenue from cold calling volatility
- Target: cold calling <40% of deal origination by Year 2

## Risk 6: Low Defensibility (Services Ceiling)

**Risk:** Omnikom remains a labor-heavy call floor; valuation caps at 2–4× EBITDA.

**Mitigation:**
- DIG data asset built from Day 1 (every call is data, not just revenue)
- SaaS subscription launched Year 2 (recurring revenue % tracked monthly)
- Platform story must be credible to investors by Month 12
- Quarterly check: "Did we move the needle on platform vs. services this quarter?"

## Risk 7: Lead Leakage

**Risk:** Revenue disappears when no one owns the next action on a signed opportunity.

**Mitigation:**
- Deal desk manager owns all open opportunities (daily review)
- n8n SLA timer fires Slack alert at every breach point
- Partner auto-reassignment at SLA breach (Section 19)
- Monthly lead leakage KPI tracked (A leads with no contact in 24h = zero-tolerance)

## Risk 8: Overbuilding Before Proof

**Risk:** Scaling callers, tech, and team before unit economics are validated.

**Mitigation:**
- Scale rule: do not add the next pod (25 callers) until current pod averages ≥8 closed deals/month for 2 consecutive months
- No Stage 2 investment until Stage 1 EBITDA is positive for 3 consecutive months
- Founder override allowed only if fundraise specifically capitalizes next stage

## Risk 9: Overseas Operation Regulatory Exposure *(NEW)*

**Risk:** Foreign-owned LLC directing US licensed activity without proper broker arrangement; DRE or DOJ classifies overseas operation as unlicensed real estate activity.

**Mitigation:**
- 3-layer entity structure strictly enforced (overseas holding → US LLC → licensed broker)
- All overseas team members: non-licensed functions ONLY (calling, data ops, CRM, AI)
- All licensed activity: US-based licensed partner or designated broker executes
- Form 5472 filed annually; FinCEN BOI filed within 90 days of LLC formation
- All contracts reviewed by California real estate attorney before execution

## Risk 10: FinCEN Enforcement on Cash Deals *(NEW)*

**Risk:** Failure to collect beneficial ownership on cash/entity purchase triggers title company refusal or FinCEN reporting failure — can kill deals and create regulatory liability.

**Mitigation:**
- n8n automation: At contract execution on any cash or entity deal → UBO collection form auto-triggered
- Deal desk manager: no commission disbursement until UBO documentation confirmed received by title
- ClosingLock for all wire instructions (encrypted; verified)
- Monthly KPI: FinCEN exceptions = zero tolerance

## Risk 11: Retell AI / TCPA Mass Liability *(NEW)*

**Risk:** AI voice agent calls numbers on DNC-registered lists. At $500–$1,500 per violation, 150K dials/month with one uncleaned list = $500K+ in TCPA liability.

**Mitigation:**
- DNC + litigator scrub is a mandatory n8n step BEFORE every Retell AI batch (non-negotiable; automated; logged)
- No Retell AI calls without scrub confirmation in the n8n workflow
- Legal reserve: $2,500/month minimum
- TCPA counsel on retainer for first 6 months of operations

## Risk 12: CRM / API Vendor Dependency *(NEW)*

**Risk:** FUB or REsimpli changes pricing, limits API access, or is acquired — breaking n8n integration and disrupting operations.

**Mitigation:**
- n8n workflows built with a GHL fallback path documented
- Never build a workflow that depends on a single CRM API without a documented alternative
- Export all CRM data monthly to a local backup (DIG is separate from CRM — it is Omnikom-owned data)
- Vendor lock-in checklist: can we migrate in 30 days if needed?
