## PART I: THE LEGAL OPERATING STRUCTURE

### 1.1 The Core Legal Problem (and Solution)

**The Problem**: An overseas person/entity cannot directly receive real estate commissions or negotiate transactions on behalf of third parties in the US without a state license.

**The Solution**: Three-layer entity structure that is 100% legal.

```
LAYER 1: Overseas Holding Entity (you)
         ↓ owns 100%
LAYER 2: US LLC (e.g., Omnikom Acquisitions LLC — Delaware or Wyoming)
         ↓ has Designated Broker as manager/contractor
LAYER 3: Licensed US Broker (the "Designated Broker")
         ↓ handles all regulated transaction activity
```

### 1.2 The Three Revenue Paths (and Which Requires What)

| Activity | License Required? | Who Does It |
|----------|:-----------------:|------------|
| Direct mail, cold calling, digital marketing | No | Overseas team / AI |
| Skip tracing, list building, data analysis | No | Overseas team / AI |
| Lead qualification calls (not acting as agent) | No | Overseas callers / AI voice agent |
| Appointment setting with sellers | No | Overseas team |
| Analyzing property data, exit path modeling | No | Overseas team / AI |
| CRM management, pipeline tracking | No | Overseas team |
| **Negotiating on behalf of a seller/buyer** | **YES** | US Licensed Broker/Agent |
| **Executing purchase agreements as agent** | **YES** | US Licensed Broker/Agent |
| **Receiving commission splits from MLS transactions** | **YES** | US Licensed Broker/Agent |
| Wholesaling (assigning your OWN contract as principal) | **No in most states** | Overseas LLC (principal) |
| Collecting assignment fees (principal-to-principal) | No | Overseas LLC |

**KEY INSIGHT — The Wholesaling Exception**: When Omnikom signs a purchase contract as the BUYER (principal, not agent), and assigns that contract for a fee, **no real estate license is required in most US states** because Omnikom is acting for its own account, not as an agent for a third party. This covers the wholesale exit path entirely without license exposure.

For exit paths requiring agency (retail listing, MLS, novation with agent representation) — the Designated Broker activates.

### 1.3 Setting Up the US Entity

**Step 1: Form the US LLC (Wyoming or Delaware preferred for foreign owners)**
- Wyoming LLC: No state income tax; strong anonymity; $100–150 formation
- Delaware LLC: Gold standard for investor credibility; $90 + $300/year franchise tax
- Use Northwest Registered Agent or IncFile ($39–150 one-time)
- **Compliance**: File FinCEN Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) within 90 days of formation — $0 fee, mandatory for foreign-owned LLCs
- File IRS Form 5472 annually (foreign-owned LLC reporting) — penalty $25,000 for late filing

**Step 2: Open US Business Banking**
- Mercury Bank (mercury.com) — best for foreign-owned US LLCs; fully online; no branch required
- Relay Financial — alternative; strong for multiple accounts/categories
- Requirement: EIN from IRS (free; apply at irs.gov; overseas applicants use Form SS-4 by fax/mail)

**Step 3: Engage a Licensed Designated Broker**

**Option A — Broker Sponsorship Model** (fastest, lowest cost)
- Spirit Real Estate (brokersponsorship.com) — $149/month; Texas-focused
- Elite Agents (eliteagents.us) — Texas LLC sponsorship, $149/month
- YourBroker (yourbroker.info) — Texas-based; $149/month + transaction fees
- **Cost**: ~$150/month + E&O insurance ($600/year) + application fee ($399 one-time)
- **Coverage**: Primarily Texas; each state requires separate licensed broker or reciprocity

**Option B — Hiring a Designated Broker as W-2 or 1099** (scales multi-state)
- Hire a broker licensed in your target states as Omnikom's "VP of Transactions"
- Compensation: $60–90K/year salary + performance bonus on closed deals
- This person becomes the licensed signatory on all contracts
- They work IN your operation (not just a nominal sponsor)

**Option C — Partner with an Existing Brokerage** (best for rapid multi-state coverage)
- Find a multi-state brokerage (eXp Realty, Real Brokerage, Fathom Realty — all 50 states)
- Structure: Omnikom provides deal flow; brokerage provides licensing umbrella
- Revenue split: 20–30% of transaction gross to brokerage; Omnikom keeps 70–80%
- Agreement type: "Marketing and Referral Services Agreement" — Omnikom provides pre-qualified leads; brokerage closes and pays Omnikom a "marketing fee" or "referral fee" — this is the key revenue path back to the overseas entity
- **This is the most scalable structure for a multi-state operation**

**eXp Realty as the broker partner**:
- Licensed in all 50 states + DC + multiple countries
- Cloud-based; no physical office required
- Broker/agent can operate virtually
- Revenue share model aligned with Omnikom's multi-tier partner network vision
- Find an eXp agent/team willing to partner: provide them deal flow in exchange for licensing umbrella + closing execution

### 1.4 The Revenue Flow (How Money Gets Back to the Overseas Entity)

```
Seller signs contract with Omnikom LLC (wholesale path)
    ↓
Omnikom LLC assigns contract to buyer for assignment fee
    ↓
Assignment fee ($5K–$30K) paid to Omnikom LLC
    ↓
Omnikom LLC distributes profit to overseas holding entity
    (no US withholding on business profits flowing to foreign LLC owner
     if properly structured; consult international CPA — 
     US-Egypt tax treaty may apply)
```

For the agent/listing path:
```
Broker closes transaction; earns commission
    ↓
Broker pays Omnikom LLC "marketing referral fee" 
    (25–30% of commission; properly documented as marketing services)
    ↓
Omnikom LLC distributes to overseas entity
```

**Tax structure**: Engage an international CPA familiar with FIRPTA, tax treaties, and foreign-owned US LLC taxation. Annual cost: $2,000–5,000. Non-negotiable.

```yaml
Agent Name: "Alex - Omnikom Seller Outreach"

Response Latency: <800ms target

System Prompt: |
  You are Alex, a friendly property solutions specialist at Omnikom.
  Your goal is to have a genuine conversation with homeowners who 
  may be open to learning about their options for selling their 
  property. You are NOT a pushy salesperson — you are a helpful 
  advisor.
  
  ALWAYS:
  - Ask for permission to continue ("Do you have 2 minutes?")
  - Listen more than you talk
  - Acknowledge their situation with empathy
  - Focus on THEIR problem, not your offer
  - Book an appointment as the ONLY goal
  
  NEVER:
  - Claim a specific price before qualification
  - Use pressure tactics
  - Ignore a DNC request (immediately say "Of course, I'll remove 
    your number right away. Have a great day.")
  - Claim to be a licensed real estate agent
  
  QUALIFICATION FLOW (LPMAMA):
  1. Confirm property ownership
  2. Ask about their situation ("What's going on with the property?")
  3. Explore timeline ("How soon are you thinking of making a move?")
  4. Soft equity probe ("Do you have a mortgage currently?")
  5. Book appointment: "Our property specialist can call you back 
     to go over your specific options — takes about 15 minutes. 
     Would tomorrow at 2pm or 4pm work?"

Webhook on call end: POST to GHL via n8n
  Payload: {

    "appointment_set": "{{appointment_time}}",
```
