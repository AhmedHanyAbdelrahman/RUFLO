# SECTION 1: STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK

## 1.1 The Inbound Thesis

Outbound calling creates cash from Day 1. Inbound creates compounding equity. By Year 3, inbound should be 15–20% of all opportunities (per the Channel Flywheel in the Master Doctrine), and it should be the *highest-converting, lowest-cost* channel because:

- A homeowner who searches "sell my inherited house fast in Riverside" has **active intent** — they are further down the decision funnel than any cold-called lead
- Inbound leads carry zero TCPA risk — they initiated contact
- Inbound content is a 24/7 asset — it works while the calling floor sleeps
- Ranking content becomes a moat — competitors cannot easily displace established authority
- AI engines citing Omnikom = third-party validation that no ad spend can buy

## 1.2 The Dual-Funnel Model

Omnikom runs two distinct inbound funnels with different content, keywords, and intake flows:

| Dimension | SELLER FUNNEL | BUYER FUNNEL |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Distressed/motivated homeowners | Retail buyers, investors, 1031 buyers, SFR funds |
| Intent keywords | "sell my house fast," "options for inherited property," "stop foreclosure options" | "off-market properties [city]," "distressed homes for sale," "wholesale deals [county]" |
| Content type | Educational, options-first, trust-building | Listing-driven, deal-flow, opportunity-access |

| Conversion goal | Booked SOR | Verified buyer (POF/pre-approval) |

## 4.2 Schema Markup (The AEO/GEO Technical Backbone)

Every content page must include structured data. This is how machines understand and cite the content.

```json
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [{
    "@type": "Question",
    "name": "What are my options if my house is in foreclosure in California?",
    "acceptedAnswer": {
      "@type": "Answer",
      "text": "In California you typically have several options: sell before
       the trustee sale, reinstate the loan, pursue a short sale, request a
       loan modification, or compare a fast as-is sale against listing. AB 2424
       (effective 2025) extends timelines, giving owners 111-266 days. The right
       path depends on equity, timeline, and goals."
    }
  }]
}
```

# SECTION 5: GEO LAYER (GENERATIVE ENGINE OPTIMIZATION)

GEO is being cited and recommended by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, and Claude. This is the newest and fastest-growing discovery channel — and most competitors are ignoring it. First-mover advantage is real here.

## 5.1 How Generative Engines Choose Sources

AI answer engines cite sources that are:
1. **Authoritative** — clear expertise, author credentials, consistent topical depth (E-E-A-T)
2. **Structured** — clean headings, lists, tables, schema markup (easy to parse and quote)
3. **Citable** — make a clear, quotable, factual claim in a self-contained sentence
4. **Fresh** — recently updated; AI engines weight recency
5. **Corroborated** — mentioned across multiple third-party sources (brand mentions matter, even unlinked)
6. **Statistically specific** — concrete numbers, data, and named sources get cited more than vague prose

## 5.2 GEO Content Tactics

| Tactic | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Quotable claims** | Write self-contained factual sentences: "In California, AB 2424 extends the foreclosure timeline to between 111 and 266 days as of 2025." AI engines lift these verbatim. |
| **Cite primary sources** | Link to CAR, DRE, county recorders, FinCEN, statutes. AI engines trust content that cites authoritative sources. |
| **Statistics with attribution** | "According to the California Association of Realtors, the 2026 median home price is forecast at $905,000." |
| **Comparison tables** | AI engines extract and reproduce tables directly. Every decision page needs a comparison table. |
| **Original data** | Publish Omnikom's own data: "Based on Omnikom's analysis of [X] California distressed sales, properties sold as-is closed in an average of 18 days." Original data = highly citable. |
| **Author E-E-A-T** | Bylines with real credentials; author bio pages; "reviewed by [licensed broker]" badges. |
| **Direct-answer format** | Lead with the answer (same 40-60 word block as AEO). Generative engines favor content that answers immediately. |
| **Updated dates** | Show "Last updated: [date]" on every page. Refresh quarterly. |

## 5.3 Platform-Specific GEO

| Engine | How to Win Citations |
|---|---|
| **Google AI Overviews** | Strong traditional SEO + schema + featured snippet wins translate directly. AI Overviews pull from top-ranking, well-structured pages. |
| **ChatGPT (Search)** | Indexes via Bing — so Bing Webmaster Tools submission is essential. Brand mentions across the web increase citation likelihood. |
| **Perplexity** | Heavily favors recent, well-sourced, citation-rich content. Publishing original data and statistics wins here. |
| **Gemini** | Tied to Google ecosystem; same signals as AI Overviews + strong Google Business Profile. |
| **Claude** | Favors clear, structured, factual content; cite primary sources. |

## 5.4 Off-Site GEO (Brand Mention Strategy)

AI engines weight how often a brand is mentioned across the web — even without links. Build mentions through:

# SECTION 8: COMPLIANCE FOR INBOUND CONTENT

Inbound content carries its own compliance requirements — especially foreclosure and "we buy houses" content.

| Risk Area | Rule | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| **CC §2945 (foreclosure content)** | No promise to "stop" or "save" from foreclosure; no implication of being a foreclosure consultant | Foreclosure pages educational only; frame as "compare options," not "we'll save your home"; include disclaimer |
| **No legal advice** | Content cannot constitute legal advice | "This is general information, not legal advice. Consult an attorney for your situation." disclaimer on all foreclosure/probate/divorce pages |
| **DRE licensing** | Cannot imply unlicensed brokerage; clarify Omnikom's role | "Omnikom compares options and connects you with licensed professionals" — clear role disclosure |
| **Truth in advertising (FTC)** | No false "cash in 24 hours" or guaranteed-price claims | All claims accurate and substantiated; ranges not guarantees |
| **Fair Housing (buyer content)** | No steering; no discriminatory language | Buyer pages reviewed for Fair Housing compliance; no demographic targeting |
| **TCPA (intake forms)** | Consent language on all forms with phone capture | "By submitting, you agree to be contacted by phone/text. Msg rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out." + checkbox |
| **CCPA (data privacy)** | California visitors can opt out of data sale | Privacy policy + cookie consent + "Do Not Sell My Info" link |
| **Review authenticity (FTC)** | No fake reviews | Only real, verifiable seller reviews |
