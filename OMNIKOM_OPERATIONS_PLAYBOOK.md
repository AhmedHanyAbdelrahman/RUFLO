# SECTION 2: CALLER STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

## 2.1 Role Definition

**Title:** Seller Discovery Representative  
**Location:** Overseas (Philippines or Egypt)  
**Core Job:** Find seller intent. Book Seller Options Reviews.

You are NOT a salesperson. You are NOT a real estate agent. You are NOT a negotiator.

You are an information gatherer and appointment setter. Your job is to find homeowners who have a property situation they need help thinking through — and book them for a free Seller Options Review with our team.

**You are never to:**
- Discuss offer prices or numbers in detail
- Negotiate any terms
- Give legal advice of any kind
- Claim to be from a bank, government agency, or lender
- Say "we can stop your foreclosure" or any similar phrase
- Pressure a homeowner to make a decision on the call

## 2.2 Pre-Call Preparation (Every Session)

Before placing a single call:

**Step 1 — Verify your list is scrubbed.**
- Confirm with your supervisor that today's list has been DNC-scrubbed and litigator-scrubbed
- If you cannot confirm: do NOT call. Notify supervisor immediately.
- A list that has not been scrubbed is a legal liability. One call to a litigator can cost $1,500.

**Step 2 — Load your list in the CRM.**
- Open REsimpli (investor/wholesale leads) or FUB (portal/inbound leads) — your supervisor will specify
- Sort by Distress Intent Score (highest first)
- Review the top 20 records before dialing — know the property address, distress type, and any previous contact notes

**Step 3 — Review your disposition codes.**
- Have the disposition codes list open (Section 2.5) before you start
- Incorrect dispositions leak revenue. A "Not Interested" should never be logged as a "DNC" unless the homeowner specifically said do not call.

**Step 4 — Check your call time.**
- No calls before 8:00 AM local time for the recipient
- No calls after 8:00 PM local time for the recipient
- Use a time zone map — California is PST/PDT; your supervisor will confirm the calling window each day

## 2.3 The Universal Calling Script

### A. Cold Opening

> "Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] calling about the property at [Address]. I'll be quick — is this a good time for just 60 seconds?"

**If yes / okay → go to B.**

**If "how did you get my number" →**
> "We work with public county property records. I'm just reaching out about [Address]. Would you have 60 seconds?"

**If "who are you / what company" →**
> "I'm with Omnikom — we help homeowners in [City/Area] compare their property options before making a decision. Quick question about [Address] — do you still own that property?"

### B. The Core Question

> "Would you consider selling that property if the numbers made sense?"

**This is the most important question you ask. Stop talking after you ask it. Wait for the answer.**

### C. If YES or MAYBE

> "Got it — would you prefer to get top dollar on the market, sell as-is without repairs, or would it help to compare both options before deciding?"

*(Whatever they say — acknowledge it.)*

> "That makes sense. What our team does is put together a free Seller Options Review — basically a comparison of what the property could net in different scenarios, so you have the full picture before you decide anything. It takes about 15 minutes, no pressure, no commitment. Are you open to doing that today or tomorrow?"

**→ If yes: book the appointment. Go to Section 2.4.**

### D. If NO

> "Totally fair. Is selling completely off the table, or just not right now?"

**If "not right now" →**
> "Understood. When would it make sense to check back in — next month, later this year, or only if the right offer came in?"

*(Log the timeframe they give you. Set the re-engagement timer in CRM.)*

**If "completely off the table" →**
> "Got it, I appreciate you telling me. I'll remove you from our list — is there a better number in case circumstances change down the road, or would you prefer we not reach out again?"

*(If they say don't call again → log as Internal DNC immediately.)*

### E. Pre-Foreclosure Safe-Harbor Branch

If you know the property has an NOD filed, OR if the homeowner mentions: foreclosure / missed payments / Notice of Default / auction / losing the house:

**STOP your standard script. Use this branch ONLY:**

> "I want to be upfront with you — we are not attorneys and this is not legal advice. We help homeowners compare their property options before making a decision. I'm not here to pressure you or make any promises. Would it be helpful to schedule a free comparison call with our team so you can see all your options?"

**What you MUST NOT say in any pre-foreclosure conversation:**
- "We can stop your foreclosure"
- "We can save your house"
- "We can pay off your debt"
- "We work with lenders to help you"
- "We are a foreclosure specialist"

These phrases trigger California CC §2945 Foreclosure Consultant Act liability. This is a felony. If you are ever unsure, say: *"Let me have our senior advisor call you back — they can walk you through everything properly."* Then flag the lead for senior ISA immediately.

### F. Objection Handling Reference

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I already have an agent" | "That's great — our review actually helps even if you're working with an agent, because we compare all the options including listing. Would it hurt to have the full picture?" |
| "I don't want to sell right now" | "No problem at all. We're not asking you to sell today. The review is just a comparison so you have the information when you're ready. Would next month be a better time to connect?" |
| "How much will you pay for it?" | "We're not buyers ourselves — we work with multiple buyers and agents in [area]. The review shows you the full range of what you could get. Want to see the comparison?" |
| "I'm not interested" | "I respect that. Is it the timing, or is selling just not something you'd consider at all?" |
| "Stop calling me" | "Absolutely — I'll remove you right now. I'm sorry for the interruption." *(Log Internal DNC immediately.)* |
| "Send me something in writing" | "Sure — what's the best email? I'll have our team send over how the review works. And I'll note that you'd like to review it before scheduling." |
| "Is this a scam?" | "I completely understand the skepticism — there are a lot of aggressive callers out there. We're Omnikom, we've helped homeowners in [City] compare their options on properties at [nearby address examples if available]. You can look us up at omnikom.com. Would you be open to a 15-minute call with our senior advisor?" |

## 2.4 Booking a Seller Options Review

When a homeowner agrees to a review:

**Required information to collect:**
1. Best phone number (confirm the one you called)
2. Best email address
3. Preferred appointment time (offer 2 specific options: "Does today at 2PM or tomorrow at 10AM work?")
4. Property address (confirm)
5. Are they the sole owner, or are there others involved? (spouse, co-owner, estate)
6. Quick motivation check: "Just so our team is prepared — is there a specific reason you're thinking about your options right now, or is this more of a general check?"

**Log in CRM immediately:**
- Stage: "Seller Options Review — Scheduled"
- Notes: Date/time booked; motivation notes; any co-owner information; distress type confirmed
- Set appointment reminder: CRM task + calendar invite to ISA

**Confirmation text (send via CRM immediately):**
> "Hi [Name], confirmed — your free Seller Options Review is scheduled for [Date] at [Time]. Our advisor [Name] will call you at [Number]. No prep needed — just 15 minutes to walk through your options. — Omnikom Team"

## 2.5 Disposition Codes — Complete Reference

Every record must be dispositioned within 5 minutes of the call ending. Incorrect dispositions are a quality failure.

| Code | Label | When to Use | Follow-Up Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SOR-BOOKED` | Seller Options Review Booked | Homeowner agreed to appointment | Set CRM task; send confirmation text; notify ISA |
| `WARM-CALLBACK` | Warm — Wants Callback | Showed interest; not ready to book yet; asked for more info | Set callback task within 24–48 hours; log motivation notes |
| `NOT-NOW-30` | Not Now — Check Back 30 Days | Selling not off table; timing not right | Set 30-day re-engagement timer in CRM |
| `NOT-NOW-60` | Not Now — Check Back 60 Days | Same as above; 60-day timeline stated | Set 60-day re-engagement timer |
| `NOT-NOW-90` | Not Now — Check Back 90 Days | Longer timeline | Set 90-day re-engagement timer; add to Nurture Network |
| `NOT-NOW-180` | Not Now — 6 Months | Selling possible but distant | Set 180-day; add to Nurture Network |
| `NOT-INTERESTED` | Not Interested | Explicitly said not interested but did not say "don't call" | No follow-up for 90 days; low priority |
| `INTERNAL-DNC` | Do Not Call — Requested | Homeowner explicitly said "don't call," "remove me," "stop calling" | Add to Internal DNC immediately; no further contact ever |
| `WRONG-NUMBER` | Wrong Number | Number does not belong to target | Remove from active list; flag for data team to re-skip |
| `NO-ANSWER` | No Answer / Voicemail | Call not answered; voicemail left or not | Schedule auto-retry at different time (max 3 attempts per number) |
| `BUSY-RETRY` | Busy / Hung Up | Call ended before conversation started | Retry once same day at different hour |
| `NOT-OWNER` | Not the Owner | Person who answered is not the property owner | Ask: "Do you have a good contact number for the owner?" Update record. |
| `LANGUAGE-BARRIER` | Language Barrier | Could not communicate effectively | Flag for bilingual caller if available |
| `DECEASED` | Deceased | Property owner is deceased | Flag for probate/estate routing; update record; sensitive handling |
| `ALREADY-LISTED` | Currently Listed | Property is active on MLS | Flag; may still qualify for options review on pricing/exit strategy |
| `ALREADY-SOLD` | Property Already Sold | Closed transaction | Remove from active list; update data |
| `QUALIFIED-SENIOR-ISA` | Escalate to Senior ISA | Highly motivated; complex situation; needs senior handler | Transfer to senior ISA queue immediately; do not lose momentum |

## 2.6 Caller Quality Standards

You are measured on quality, not volume. The North Star metric is **net profit per seller conversation** — not dials.

**Weekly quality targets (Base expectation):**
- Qualified conversation rate: ≥15% of total conversations
- SOR booking rate: ≥20% of qualified conversations
- Completed SOR rate: ≥65% of booked SORs
- DNC compliance: 100% — zero violations

**Bonus structure:**
- $25 per Seller Options Review that completes (confirmed by ISA)
- $100 per signed opportunity (confirmed by deal desk)
- $250 per closed deal originating from your qualified lead
- Quality gate: Must maintain ≥15% qualified conversation rate to remain bonus-eligible

**Performance review triggers:**
- Below 15% qualified conversation rate for 2 consecutive weeks → coaching session required
- Below 10% for 3 consecutive weeks → corrective action plan
- Any DNC violation → immediate coaching; second violation → suspension pending review
- Any CC §2945 violation (foreclosure language) → immediate suspension; legal review required

# SECTION 3: SENIOR ISA / SELLER OPTIONS REVIEW SOP

## 3.1 Role Definition

**Title:** Senior ISA / Seller Conversion Representative  
**Location:** Overseas  
**Core Job:** Convert "maybe" into a completed Seller Options Review. Convert a completed SOR into a signed opportunity.

You receive warm leads — homeowners who have already expressed interest. Your job is to deliver the Seller Options Review, build trust, gather the information needed to route the opportunity, and get the homeowner to agree to move forward with the best exit path.

**You are not licensed and you do not:**
- Quote a specific purchase price as a firm offer
- Sign or prepare contracts
- Negotiate final terms
- Provide legal advice
- Represent the homeowner or any buyer

## 3.2 Pre-SOR Preparation

Before every Seller Options Review appointment:

**Step 1 — Review the lead record.**
- Read all caller notes and AI call summary
- Note: distress type; motivation score; stated exit preference; any objections raised; co-owner status
- If pre-foreclosure: re-read CC §2945 safe harbor language before the call

**Step 2 — Pull property data.**
- PropStream AVM: estimated value; equity; mortgage balance; ownership tenure
- PropertyRadar: confirm any court filings (NOD; probate; divorce)
- Google Street View: quick visual on condition
- Notes: "What is the as-is range? What is the retail range? What is the gap?"

**Step 3 — Prepare the comparison.**
Before the call, draft in your notes:
- **Retail estimate:** estimated list price × 94% (avg CA close-to-list ratio) − 5–6% commission − estimated repair/prep cost = estimated net to seller
- **As-is estimate:** retail value × 70–80% (distressed discount) = investor offer range; Omnikom 40% of the spread
- **Timeline comparison:** retail: 45–90 days; as-is: 14–30 days

## 3.3 The Seller Options Review Conversation

**Duration target:** 15–20 minutes  
**Tone:** Advisor, not salesperson. Helpful, not pushy.

### Opening (2 minutes)

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from Omnikom. Thanks for taking the time — I have everything ready for your comparison. Before I walk you through the numbers, can I ask a quick question? Is your main priority getting the most money possible, getting it done quickly and cleanly, or somewhere in between?"

*(Their answer shapes which exit path you lead with.)*

### Property Confirmation (2 minutes)

> "Great. I'm looking at [Address] — [# beds / # baths / approx sqft if known]. Just to make sure I have accurate numbers — is the property currently occupied? And are there any significant repairs or conditions I should know about?"

*(Key: condition affects the as-is discount; occupancy affects timeline.)*

> "And just to confirm — are you the sole decision-maker on this, or is there anyone else involved, like a co-owner or family member?"

*(If co-owner: "Would they be part of this conversation or is this your decision to make?")*

### The Seller Options Review — Delivering the Comparison (8–10 minutes)

> "Here's what I found for [Address]. Let me walk you through three scenarios so you have the full picture."

**Scenario 1: Retail Listing**
> "If you listed the property with an agent, based on comparable sales in [City/Zip], you'd be looking at a list price in the [range] range. After agent commissions, closing costs, and any prep work, your estimated net would be approximately [net range]. Timeline is typically 45 to 90 days, sometimes longer depending on the market."

**Scenario 2: As-Is Sale (Investor Route)**
> "If you wanted to skip repairs, showings, and the traditional process entirely — selling as-is to a cash buyer — the offer range would be approximately [as-is range]. You'd net less per dollar, but the process is 14 to 30 days, no repairs, no showings, no contingencies."

**Scenario 3: The Middle Path (Novation / Creative)**
> "There's also a middle option that not everyone knows about — where a partner handles the marketing and sale process without you having to do anything, and you capture more of the retail price than an as-is sale while still avoiding all the prep work. This path typically nets [novation net range]. It takes a bit longer — 60 to 90 days — but it's worth knowing it exists."

*(Only present Scenario 3 if the property is a good novation candidate — equity ≥30%, condition allows cosmetic prep, market is active.)*

**Summary:**
> "So to summarize: listing gets you the most — around [retail net] — but takes the most time and effort. As-is is the cleanest — around [as-is net] — with the least hassle. And the middle path could get you close to retail without the preparation headaches. Which of those sounds most aligned with what you're looking for?"

### Routing and Close (3–5 minutes)

Based on their answer:

**If they lean retail:**
> "Given that, I'd recommend connecting you with one of our certified listing partners in [Area] — they specialize in exactly this type of property. If the numbers make sense after their market analysis, would you be open to meeting with them this week?"

→ Route to listing agent partner. Assign per partner scorecard.

**If they lean as-is:**
> "For an as-is offer, I can have one of our investment partners put together a specific number for you within 24 hours. Would that be helpful — just so you have a real offer to compare against the listing option?"

→ Route to investor partner. Set 24-hour offer SLA.

**If they lean novation / middle path:**
> "For the middle-path option, I'd want to connect you with one of our specialists who works with exactly this situation. Can I have them reach out to you tomorrow morning?"

→ Route to novation operator. Verify licensure before assignment.

**If undecided:**
> "That makes total sense — you don't have to decide anything today. What I can do is set up a brief call with one of our senior advisors who handles all three paths, so you can ask any questions before deciding. Would Thursday or Friday work?"

### Handling No-Shows

If a homeowner does not show for a booked SOR:

**Within 30 minutes of missed appointment:**
1. Send text: *"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from Omnikom — we had a call scheduled at [Time]. No worries if something came up. Would [Tomorrow at Time] work to reschedule, or is there a better time?"*
2. Make one call attempt
3. Log in CRM: `SOR-NOSHOW` → set 24-hour re-engagement task
4. If no response in 24 hours: move to `WARM-CALLBACK` → add to 7-day AI nurture sequence

## 3.4 Information to Log After Every SOR (Mandatory)

After every completed SOR, log in CRM within 30 minutes:

- **Motivation score (1–10):** Where 1 = casual curiosity; 10 = must sell within 30 days
- **Timeline stated:** When do they want to make a decision?
- **Exit preference:** Retail / as-is / novation / undecided
- **Primary objection (if any):** Price / timing / family / repairs / tenant / legal
- **Co-owner status:** Sole / joint (spouse/family/partner)
- **Condition notes:** Any significant repair issues or upgrades mentioned
- **Follow-up action:** What is the exact next step and who owns it?
- **Distress confirmed:** NOD / probate / divorce / tax delinquent / vacant (check all that apply)
- **AI summary:** Paste the AI-generated call summary into notes (Retell AI auto-generates if call was via AI; ISA writes manually if phone call)
- **Revenue path:** Which exit path + which partner is this being routed to?

# SECTION 4: DEAL DESK MANAGER SOP

## 4.1 Role Definition

**Title:** Deal Desk Manager  
**Location:** US-based (remote acceptable)  
**Core Job:** No opportunity slips. Every signed opportunity moves toward a closed deal. Revenue integrity.

You are the bridge between the overseas team and the US partner network. You own every opportunity from the moment it is signed until it closes or is officially dead.

## 4.2 Daily Deal Desk Workflow

### Morning Review (9:00–9:30 AM EST)

**Run the following checks every morning:**

**1 — Hot Lead Alert Review (Slack #deals-hot)**
- Any A lead (Score 80+) assigned since yesterday → confirm partner contact made within 5 minutes
- Any A lead with no partner contact confirmation → immediately call the partner; if no response in 10 minutes → reassign to secondary partner

**2 — Open Signed Opportunities Aging Report**
- Any signed opportunity with no status update in 72 hours → immediate outreach to partner
- Any signed opportunity stalled at "offer pending" for >7 days → escalation call
- Any signed opportunity stalled at "under contract" for >30 days → title/escrow status check

**3 — Today's SOR Appointments**
- Review all SORs scheduled for today → confirm ISA is prepared and record is fully enriched
- Any SOR with incomplete property data → pull PropStream AVM before appointment time

**4 — FinCEN / Compliance Checkpoint**
- Any deals moving toward close today → confirm UBO documentation is complete and delivered to title
- Any deals with entity buyers (LLC, trust, corporation) → FinCEN checklist must be completed before close

**5 — Partner SLA Status**
- Check REsimpli + FUB for any partner-assigned leads with no CRM update in 72 hours
- Log SLA breach in partner scorecard

### Signed Opportunity Management

For every signed opportunity in the pipeline, you own the following checklist:

**Day 1 — Assignment:**
☐ Confirm partner accepted the assignment (verbal or CRM confirmation)  
☐ Confirm partner has full seller information (contact; property; motivation notes; ISA summary)  
☐ Set SLA timer: 24-hour CMA/offer delivery expectation  
☐ Log: `ASSIGNED — [Partner Name] — [Date]` in CRM  
☐ Slack notification to #deals-pipeline: "New signed opportunity: [Address] → [Partner]"

**Day 2 — Offer/CMA Confirmation:**
☐ Partner delivered CMA or offer? If yes → log and route to seller via ISA  
☐ If no → call partner; escalate if no response within 2 hours  
☐ Log: CMA/offer amount; partner contact notes

**Day 3–7 — Seller Decision:**
☐ ISA following up with seller on offer/CMA?  
☐ Any seller objections or counter-requests → manage through ISA; escalate to licensed partner for negotiation  
☐ If seller accepts → begin contract preparation by licensed partner

**Under Contract — Weekly Check:**
☐ Title opened?  
☐ Earnest money deposited?  
☐ Inspection completed / waived?  
☐ FinCEN UBO form delivered to title?  
☐ Closing date confirmed?  
☐ Wire instructions sent via ClosingLock?

**Closing:**
☐ Closing confirmed by title  
☐ Funds received by Omnikom US LLC  
☐ Partner payment disbursed (per signed agreement)  
☐ Deal logged in DIG (outcome data; exit type; Omnikom net; partner; county)  
☐ Seller satisfaction survey sent  
☐ Case study notes captured (for SEO + investor proof)  
☐ Slack: #deals-closed: "[Address] CLOSED — [Exit Type] — [Omnikom Net] — [Partner]"

## 4.3 FinCEN Cash Deal Protocol

For ANY deal where the buyer is paying cash OR the buyer is an entity (LLC, trust, corporation, partnership):

**Step 1 — Trigger UBO form at contract execution**
- n8n automation should do this automatically
- If not triggered: manually send ClosingLock UBO collection form to the buyer

**Step 2 — Collect from the buyer:**
- Full legal name of all beneficial owners with 25%+ interest
- Date of birth
- Current residential address
- Government-issued ID (passport or driver's license — copy)

**Step 3 — Deliver to title company:**
- Send complete UBO package to title company escrow officer within 24 hours of contract execution
- Get written confirmation from title that they received it
- Log in CRM: `FINCEN-COMPLETE — [Title Company] — [Date]`

**Step 4 — No close without confirmation**
- Do NOT authorize any commission disbursement or closing until title confirms UBO was received
- If title refuses to close without additional documentation → escalate to Omnikom legal immediately

**Zero tolerance: Any cash/entity deal that closes without documented FinCEN UBO collection is a regulatory violation.**

## 4.4 Partner SLA Enforcement

When a partner breaches an SLA:

| Breach | Your Action |
|---|---|
| A lead: No contact in 5 minutes | Call partner directly. If no answer in 10 min → reassign + Slack alert |
| A lead: No offer in 24 hours | Call partner. If no response in 2 hours → reassign; log SLA breach |
| Any lead: No CRM update in 72 hours | Email + call. If no response in 24 hours → suspend new lead flow to that partner |
| 3 breaches in 30 days | Partner status review meeting. Downgrade tier if not resolved |
| 5 breaches in 60 days | Partner suspended. Leads reallocated. Formal notice sent. |

**Log every SLA breach in the Partner Scorecard (Section 6.3).**

## 4.5 Revenue Integrity Checks

**Weekly (every Monday):**
- Reconcile all closed deals against commission agreements
- Verify all partner payments match signed 60/40 agreements
- Confirm no deal closed without a signed partner agreement in place
- Check: any deal where Omnikom's 40% was not collected → immediate escalation

**Monthly:**
- Revenue by exit type (wholesale vs. listing vs. novation vs. flip)
- Revenue by partner (top 5; bottom 5; any concentration risk >25%?)
- Revenue by county (which counties are producing? which are underperforming?)
- Revenue by data source (PropertyRadar vs. PropStream vs. REDX vs. DealMachine)
- Recurring revenue % of total (SaaS + subscriptions — must grow every month)

## 7.2 DNC Management Protocol

**Federal DNC:** Scrub every list before use. Download monthly update from FTC DNC Registry. n8n automation handles this; confirm log.

**State DNC:** California does not have a separate state DNC list — Federal DNC applies. Some states (IN, WI, TX, FL) do have state lists — scrub state lists when expanding outside CA.

**Internal DNC:** Omnikom's internal list of homeowners who have requested not to be contacted. This is SEPARATE from the Federal DNC — it includes people who said "don't call" even if they are not on the Federal registry.

**Internal DNC Rules:**
- Any homeowner who says "stop calling," "don't call," "remove me," or similar → added to Internal DNC IMMEDIATELY by the caller who received the request
- Internal DNC is never overridden — not by a score, not by a good list, not by a manager
- Internal DNC is reviewed monthly: any numbers that are also on Federal DNC → confirm both lists updated
- Internal DNC is loaded into REsimpli and FUB as suppression lists — no lead can be created for a number on Internal DNC

## 7.3 TCPA Compliance Protocol for Retell AI

Retell AI uses automated outbound calling. ATDS (Automated Telephone Dialing System) rules apply.

**Before every Retell AI batch:**
1. Confirm list was pulled from public records (county data — these are property owners, not consumers who opted into a marketing list)
2. Confirm DNC scrub completed (n8n log)
3. Confirm litigator scrub completed
4. Confirm calling window is within 8AM–8PM local time for the recipient's time zone
5. Confirm no wireless numbers are being called without prior express written consent

**If a Retell AI call is challenged:**
- Do NOT ignore it
- Immediately escalate to deal desk manager
- Preserve the call recording and all related logs
- Do not re-dial the number pending legal review
- Log the incident in the Compliance Incident Register

## 7.4 California Two-Party Consent for Call Recording

California is an all-party consent state. All calls must be recorded only with consent from all parties.

**Protocol for recorded calls:**
- Retell AI opens with a disclosure: "This call may be recorded for quality and training purposes."
- Human callers: script includes opening: "I just want to let you know this call may be recorded."
- If homeowner objects to recording: stop recording immediately; make note; continue call
- If homeowner hangs up upon hearing recording disclosure: log as `DECLINED-RECORDING`; do not call back on the same list

## 7.5 Pre-Foreclosure (CC §2945) Compliance Log

For every call made to a homeowner with a confirmed NOD:

Maintain a log entry with:
- Property address
- Homeowner name
- Date of call
- Caller name
- Script branch used (standard vs. safe-harbor)
- Any mention of foreclosure, default, or financial hardship by the homeowner
- Disposition
- Any agreement made or implied

This log is the defense document if a CC §2945 complaint is ever filed. It shows that Omnikom's callers provided information only — not foreclosure consultant services.

## 7.6 Partner Payment Compliance Check

Before disbursing any payment to a partner:

☐ Signed partner agreement on file  
☐ Specific deal identified in the agreement or a deal-specific addendum  
☐ Verify partner is not a licensed settlement service provider on this transaction (RESPA §8 check)  
☐ If partner is a licensed attorney: confirm compensation is structured as reciprocal referral, not cash fee (RPC 7.2(b))  
☐ W-9 / W-8BEN on file  
☐ Closed deal confirmation from title (HUD-1 or Closing Disclosure with Omnikom proceeds confirmed)  
☐ Wire instructions received via ClosingLock (not email)  

**Zero payment without a signed agreement and closed deal confirmation.**
